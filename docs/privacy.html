Ticker Battle Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 October 2026.

The short version: Ticker Battle doesn't collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no server of its own, no analytics and no tracking. What you do in the app stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • Your duel name and avatar, your duel history, play balance, tokens and cosmetics.
  • Your settings, such as the demo-prices switch.

Delete the app to remove all of it. CLEAR HISTORY in the app erases your history.

What moves between phones

Phones in a duel swap QR codes in person, with the camera: your duel name and avatar number, your picks (hidden on screen until the reveal), the room settings and the start time. Nothing is sent over the internet to do this. Anyone who decodes a QR code can read what's in it, so don't put anything private in your duel name.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan duel QR codes. Images aren't saved or sent anywhere.

Price and rate files

To score duels, the app downloads public files from GitHub Pages (toussaint222.github.io): daily stock prices from IEX's free historical data, and the SOFR rate published by the Federal Reserve Bank of New York. The requests contain no account, identifier or personal data. GitHub, which hosts the files, receives your device's IP address as part of any web request and handles it under GitHub's privacy statement.

Purchases

The one in-app purchase ("Starter pack") is handled by Apple. We don't receive your payment details; the app only learns whether you own it.

Ads

This version shows no ads.

Children

Ticker Battle is a game with play money. It doesn't knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Play money only

Entertainment only. Not financial advice. No real money: the play balance and tokens can't be bought (beyond the one starter pack), cashed out or transferred.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: email toussaint@mail.grokbot.com or see the support page.
