Ticker Battle Support

Last updated 30 October 2026.

Need help or found a bug? Email toussaint@mail.grokbot.com. Tell us your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened.

Is any real money involved?

No. Ticker Battle is a game. Your balance is play money, and tokens buy avatar looks only. Neither can be cashed out, transferred or exchanged for anything of value. Entertainment only. Not financial advice.

How does scoring work?

Each player's score is the average % return of their picks over the duel window: (end price − start price) ÷ start price, averaged across picks. Real duels are scored from IEX's free historical price files, which are published after each trading day. So results arrive on the next trading day, and the app shows RESULTS LOADING until the prices are in. Turn on notifications to hear when they're ready.

What are demo prices?

Demo prices are sample prices that settle right away, so you can try a duel at any hour, with a computer player if you like. Turn them on in the app's About screen or in the iOS Settings app under Ticker Battle. Every phone in a duel needs the same setting.

What are Bonds (SOFR)?

Optionally, you can put part of your duel in bonds. That part earns the SOFR (Secured Overnight Financing Rate), published each business day by the Federal Reserve Bank of New York, for the duel's length, instead of moving with stocks. Play money only.

I bought the Starter pack. How do I restore it?

The Starter pack is a one-time purchase that adds 100 tokens. On a new or reset phone, tap RESTORE PURCHASES in the Avatar Arcade or in About, signed in with the same Apple Account. Restoring brings back the purchase; it never adds the 100 tokens twice to the same save.
